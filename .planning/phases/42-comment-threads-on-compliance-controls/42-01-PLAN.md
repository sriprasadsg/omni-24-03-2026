<objective>
Build the backend core of tenant-scoped control comments: a service module that inserts/lists comments against a brand-new `control_comments` collection (never embedded on the tenant-exempt `compliance_controls` document), a router that role-gates POST to `admin`/`super_admin`/`compliance_reviewer` (D-01) while leaving GET open to any authenticated tenant user (A2), and registration of that router in `router_registry.py` so the routes actually resolve in the live app.

Purpose: Satisfies Success Criteria 1 (post + retrieve), 2 (tenant isolation), and 4 (persistence/retrievable) at the API/data tier. No PATCH/DELETE route is created — D-03 immutability is enforced by omission.
Output: `control_comments_service.py`, `control_comments_endpoints.py`, router registration, and `test_control_comments.py` covering the role gate, post+list, and tenant isolation.
</objective>
