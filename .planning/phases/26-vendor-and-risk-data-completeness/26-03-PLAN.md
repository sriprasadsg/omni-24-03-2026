<objective>
Deliver the backend half of RISK-01: the risk register distinguishes inherent (pre-mitigation) from residual (post-mitigation) risk, additively, without renaming or removing the existing blended `risk_score`.

Purpose: Today `risk_service.py` computes a single `risk_score = likelihood * impact`. RISK-01 needs a residual score alongside it. The codebase's consistent convention (STATE.md decisions 02-01 through 10-02) is additive-only field extension — a rename would silently break `RiskRegister.tsx`'s four `risk_score` reads (Pitfall 3).

Output: additive residual fields in `create_risk`/`update_risk`, range-validated optional `residual_likelihood`/`residual_impact` on the request models, and `backend/tests/test_risk_inherent_residual.py`. No new dependencies. FAIR-style quantification is explicitly out of scope (that is Phase 31) — plain `likelihood * impact`, done twice.
</objective>
