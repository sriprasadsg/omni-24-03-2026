<objective>
Deliver the backend half of VRISK-02: a vendor record can list, add, and remove its own subprocessors, stored as an audit-friendly sub-array on the existing `vendors` document using the same `$push`/`$pull` pattern already proven by `add_assessment`/`add_document`.

Purpose: Subprocessor discovery does not exist at all today. This makes it trackable data on the vendor record (the vendor detail view in 26-04 consumes it), following the sibling sub-resource pattern rather than the unused generic `update_vendor()` (whose blanket `$set` would overwrite the whole array).

Output: two service methods on `VendorService`, a `SubprocessorCreate` model and three routes in `vendor_endpoints.py`, and `backend/tests/test_vendor_subprocessors.py`. No new dependencies, no new collection — subprocessors live inside the vendor doc.
</objective>
