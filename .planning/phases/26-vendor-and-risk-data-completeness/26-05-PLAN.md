<objective>
Deliver the frontend half of RISK-01: surface the residual (post-mitigation) risk score alongside the existing inherent/blended score in the risk register, and let a user enter residual likelihood/impact when creating a risk.

Purpose: `RiskRegister.tsx` renders a single `risk_score` (now the inherent score after 26-03). RISK-01 needs the residual score visible too. Output: a new Residual Score column in `RiskRegister.tsx` and two residual inputs in `RiskFormModal.tsx`. Scoped to these two components only (they define their interfaces locally and pass data through `api.createRisk`, which is untyped) — no types.ts or apiService edits.
</objective>
