<objective>
Make startup mirror the single table into the shared role documents — all 11 built-in roles,
including `admin` and the three ITAM roles — without ever touching a tenant's own role documents,
and make the two remaining session-minting responses (signup and MFA verify-login) deliver the
resolver's permissions while signup stops writing a per-user permissions array.

Purpose: ACC-02 requires the missing ITAM role documents to exist (SC4) and the seed lists in
`app_startup.py` to stop being a second and third copy of the truth. The current seed matches on
`{"name": X}` alone under the "platform-admin" context, which 75-RESEARCH reproduced overwriting a
tenant's own same-named document. ACC-01/D-24 require every response that carries
`user.permissions` to come from the resolver.

Output: a single seed loop in `backend/app_startup.py`, signup and MFA responses wired to
`session_permissions`, and `backend/tests/test_role_seeding.py`.
</objective>
