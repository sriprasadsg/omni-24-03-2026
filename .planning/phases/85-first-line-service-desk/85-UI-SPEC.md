# Phase 85 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the First-Line Service Desk. Seven surfaces: (1) a **Service desk** settings card on the Autonomous Ops screen (switch Off / Drafts only / Post answers, readiness list, tenant words, status line, breaker restore); (2) the **staff draft panel** on a ticket (Send as is / Edit and send / Discard); (3) the **Reply to requester** choice and the visibility marks on comments; (4) the **requester answer panel** on a portal ticket (This fixed it / I still need help, AI-assisted mark, cited articles, hand-off and withdrawn notices); (5) **Activity rows** and Undo for desk decisions; (6) the **Knowledge Base** additions (Help desk toggle, article edit dialog, Knowledge gaps tab, Add starter IT articles); (7) the **tray "My tickets" window** (PowerShell WinForms, plain-terms layout, section 8). Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Locked inputs: 85-CONTEXT.md D-01..D-37. Nothing here re-opens them. Details the context left open are recorded as researcher defaults in "Assumptions Recorded" (IDs R1..R14); the planner treats them as decided unless the user overrules.
> Extends, does not replace, `81-UI-SPEC.md`, `83-UI-SPEC.md` and `84-UI-SPEC.md`: tokens, the four-size and two-weight type scale, the spacing scale, chip anatomy and tones, `.card`, `Modal` conventions, `EmptyStateCard`, `HealthNotice` blocks, the Undo-in-Result-cell rule, the stop and shadow rules and the settings-card anatomy (83 section 6, `AutonomousOpsResponseSettings.tsx`) are inherited as written. Only deltas are declared here. The tray window cannot use web tokens and is specified in its own terms.
> New infrastructure: none. No new library, token, colour or chip tone. Files read for this contract: `components/AutonomousOpsResponseSettings.tsx`, `AutonomousOpsResponseSettingsDialogs.tsx`, `AutonomousOpsActivityList.tsx`, `AutonomousOpsHealthChips.tsx`, `AutonomousOpsHealthCards.tsx`, `InternalTicketsDashboard.tsx` (status meta, comments tab, lines 60-115 and 1318-1375), `KnowledgeBaseDashboard.tsx`, `Modal.tsx`, `icons.tsx`, `agent-install/omni-agent-rs/scripts/tray_icon.ps1`, `shared/autonomous-ops-banned-terms.json`.

## Spacing Scale

Inherited unchanged from 81-UI-SPEC (4, 8, 16, 24, 32, 48; 64 unused). Phase 85 usage:

| Token | Tailwind | Usage in this phase |
|-------|----------|---------------------|
| xs 4px | `1` | Icon-to-text gap in chips and buttons, gap between a title and its meta line |
| sm 8px | `2` | Gap between chips, between the buttons of one row, between stacked helper lines |
| md 16px | `4` | Panel padding inside the draft and requester panels (`p-4` blocks), gap between option cards, form row gap |
| lg 24px | `6` | Card padding (`.card p-6`, panel header `px-6 py-4`), gap between sections inside a card, dialog gaps |
| xl 32px | `8` | Empty-state card padding (unchanged) |
| 2xl 48px | `12` | Empty-state icon circle (unchanged) |

Exceptions (all multiples of 4):
- `Send as is`, `Edit and send`, `Discard`, `Send edit`, `This fixed it`, `I still need help`, `Save service desk settings`, `Restore Post answers`, `Write article`, `Dismiss` and every dialog confirm are `min-h-10` (40px, `px-4`), the consequential-control rule.
- Mode option cards: `min-h-10` hit area is the whole card (`p-4`); the three cards sit in `grid gap-4 lg:grid-cols-3`, stacked below `lg`.
- Reply-to-requester choice: two radio rows `min-h-10` (40px), the whole row is the hit area.
- Word chip remove button: `h-8 w-8` (32px hit area), 16px icon (the 83 service chip rule).
- Draft textarea `min-h-40` (160px), `rows={8}`; requester note textarea `min-h-24` (96px), `rows={3}`.
- Cited-article disclosure buttons `min-h-10`; expanded article text region `max-h-64` (256px) with `overflow-y-auto`.
- Gap group card: `p-4` inside the list, `space-y-4` between cards.
- No half-steps in new elements.

## Color

Inherited from 81, 83 and 84: dominant `--bg-main`, secondary `.card` surface, accent `primary-600`, destructive `red-600`, amber warning, blue info, neutral text. 60/30/10 unchanged: the card, panels and dialogs are `.card`/`Modal` surfaces; accent stays small.

No tone is added. Phase 85 meanings map onto existing chips (anatomy from 81: 12px `aria-hidden` icon plus visible text):

| Meaning | Chip | Tone and icon |
|---------|------|---------------|
| A draft waiting for staff, staff only | `Draft` | BLUE, `LockIcon` (the Suggested anatomy) |
| An AI answer the requester can see | `AI-assisted` | NEUTRAL, `SparklesIcon` |
| Visible to the requester (staff reply) | `Sent to requester` | NEUTRAL, `EyeIcon` |
| An answer that was withdrawn | `Withdrawn` | OUTLINE, `XCircleIcon` |
| Local model reachable / not reachable / not checked | `Online` / `Offline` / `Not checked` | GREEN `CheckCircleIcon` / RED `XCircleIcon` / OUTLINE `InfoIcon` |
| Posting will happen / answers are drafts for now | `Posting` / `Drafts for now` | GREEN `CheckCircleIcon` / AMBER `ClockIcon` |
| Article is used for ticket answers | `Help desk` | BLUE, `SparklesIcon` |
| Article copied from the platform's starter set | `Starter copy` | OUTLINE, `FileTextIcon` |
| Platform reference article (never cited) | `Platform reference` | OUTLINE, `LockIcon` |
| Ticket raised by automation, or no requester | no chip | n/a |
| Activity rows | the 81 chips (`Done`, `Suggested`, `Undone`, `Refused`, `Failed`, `Simulation`) | as 81 |

Accent reserved for (the 81 list items 1 to 4, 82 item 5, 83 items 6 to 9 and 84 items 10 to 13 stand; Phase 85 adds exactly five):
14. `Send as is` and, in edit mode, `Send edit`: the one affirmative action of the staff draft panel (only one of the two is visible at a time).
15. `This fixed it` on the requester panel.
16. `Save service desk settings` when the form has unsaved changes.
17. The confirm of a dialog that lifts a safeguard: `Post answers` (in `Post answers to requesters?`) and `Restore Post answers`; the same pattern as `Resume automatic actions`.
18. `Add articles` in `Add starter IT articles?`.

Accent is NOT used for: `Edit and send`, `Discard`, `I still need help`, `Write article`, `Dismiss`, `Restore`, `Open ticket`, the selected mode option (selection is a 2px neutral-dark border plus `CheckCircleIcon`, so Post answers never looks like the "safe" default), `Retry`, any chip, any disclosure, or section headings.

Destructive red (button) is used for exactly one confirm: `Discard draft` (the draft cannot be regenerated, there is no second AI attempt). Red also marks the `Offline` chip, inline error text and error blocks. Nothing else is red. Blockers and readiness gaps are amber `role="status"` text, not red: they are a to-do, not a failure.

Meaning never relies on colour alone: every chip carries an icon and visible text; the mode group states the selected option in words; a disabled button carries a visible reason line referenced by `aria-describedby`.

### 2. Service desk settings card (DESK-02, DESK-05, D-03, D-22, D-23, D-25)

A collapsed `.card p-6`, same anatomy as `Response settings` (83 section 6): `<h2>` 18/600 containing a `<button aria-expanded aria-controls>` with `TicketIcon` 20px, text `Service desk`, `ChevronRightIcon` that rotates; under it a 14/400 muted summary line, e.g. `Drafts only · Local model online · Typical answer time 1 min 40 s · 2 extra words`. Variants: `Off`, `Post answers (posting)`, `Post answers (drafts for now)`, `Drafts only (restore needed)`, and the status part `Local model offline` or `Answer time not measured`. Loads on mount (one fetch), refetches on expand and every 60 s while expanded; no polling while collapsed.

Expanded body (`border-t pt-6`), top to bottom, one staged form with one Save (settings changes are recorded in the ledger and audit chain by the server, 83 D-22):

**a. Breaker block** (only when the desk breaker tripped, D-25). Amber `HealthNotice` (`PauseCircleIcon`, `role="status"`): `The service desk dropped to Drafts only. 3 answers were withdrawn within 24 hours. Answers stay drafts until someone restores Post answers.` Under it, 12/400 muted: `A requester choosing "I still need help" never counts toward this.` Button `Restore Post answers` (secondary, `min-h-10`), rendered only with `canRestore`, opens the restore dialog. While tripped, the `Post answers` option is disabled (the restore button is the only way back) with the reason line `Restore Post answers first.`

**b. Status line** (`role="status"`, always visible when expanded): a chip `Online` (GREEN), `Offline` (RED) or `Not checked` (OUTLINE) then 14/400 text.
- Offline: `Offline: the local model isn't running` and under it 12/400 muted `Every ticket goes to a person and is marked "service desk offline" until it is back. Ticket text never leaves this server.`
- Online: `The local model is running.` The model tag follows in 12/400 mono muted (`llama3.2:3b`).
- Typical answer time (DESK-05): 14/400 `Typical answer time: 1 min 40 s` with 12/400 muted `Median of the last {n} answers.`; no data: `Answer time not measured yet.` with muted `Timeouts use safe defaults until an administrator measures this server.` With more than 5 answers measured the muted line is `Median of the last {n} answers.`
- Queue limit line (12/400 muted): `An answer that isn't ready 10 minutes after the ticket arrives is dropped and the ticket goes to a person.`

**c. ### 3. Staff draft panel and Reply to requester (DESK-01, D-09, D-10, D-11)

**Draft panel.** A `.card overflow-hidden` section placed directly under the ticket header and above the tab bar of the detail pane (so it is visible on every tab), rendered only while a draft is waiting and `canActOnDraft`. `<section aria-labelledby>` with `id="service-desk-draft"`, `tabIndex={-1}`. The ticket list row and the header of the detail pane also show the BLUE chip `Draft waiting` (`SparklesIcon`) so staff can find tickets with drafts.

Zones:
1. **Header** (`flex flex-wrap items-start justify-between gap-4 px-6 py-4 border-b`): left `SparklesIcon` 20px, then 14/600 `Draft answer` and 12/400 muted `Written by the service desk {relative} from {n} articles. Only staff can see this.`; right the `Draft` chip (BLUE, `LockIcon`).
2. **Why a draft** (only when the server supplies `draftReason`, 12/400 muted, `InfoIcon` 12px, under the header): `Kept as a draft because Autonomous Ops is at Suggest.` / `... because this organization is in shadow mode.` / `... because the service desk is set to Drafts only.` / `... because the hourly limit of 20 answers was reached.` / `... because automatic actions are stopped.` / `... because the service desk dropped to Drafts only.` / `... because this computer's tray app can't show AI answers yet.`
3. **Answer** (`px-6 py-4`): 12/600 label `What the requester will see`; then a bordered `rounded-lg p-4` box: first line 12/600 with `SparklesIcon`: `AI-assisted answer from your IT knowledge base`; answer text 14/400 `whitespace-pre-wrap break-words`; then 12/600 `Based on` followed by `CitedArticles`; then the fixed footer 12/400 muted `Not right? Destructive and consequential actions in this phase, and the confirmation approach for each:
- **Send as is / Edit and send**: one click by design. Safeguards instead of a dialog: the consequence line before the buttons names the requester and the new ticket status, the answer text and citations are on screen, sending is a person's recorded choice, the server refuses it when a person already replied or the ticket is closed.
- **Discard**: dialog (red confirm), because the desk makes one attempt per ticket.
- **Post answers** (switch change): dialog with the readiness list; **Restore Post answers**: dialog.
- **Undo** a posted answer: one click, no dialog (83 rule); the hint states what happens; recorded in the ledger and counted toward the desk breaker.
- **This fixed it**: one click; the line under the button states `Marks your ticket resolved.`; a person can reopen the ticket.
- **Dismiss** a gap: one click, restorable from `Show dismissed`.
- **Add starter IT articles**: dialog, because it writes articles into the knowledge base.
- Not destructive: Save settings (other values), Help desk switch (reversible), I still need help.
