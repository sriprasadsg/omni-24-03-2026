# Phase 30: AI Questionnaire Auto-Answer - Summary

## Overview
Phase 30 implements the AI-assisted Questionnaire Auto-Answer feature, enabling automated drafting of questionnaire responses and ensuring a mandatory human review process.

## Summary of Plan Waves

*   **30-01-PLAN.md (RAG-01: Auto-Drafting Service):** Establishes the backend infrastructure for the auto-drafting service.
*   **30-02-PLAN.md (RAG-02: Human Review Workflow):** Implements the mandatory human review gate for AI-generated answers.
*   **30-03-PLAN.md (RAG-03: Questionnaire Dashboard Extension):** Enhances the dashboard to support questionnaire management and review status tracking.
*   **30-04-PLAN.md (RAG-04: Tenant Isolation and Compliance):** Ensures compliance and security via tenant-isolated data access for AI models.
*   **30-05-PLAN.md (RAG-05: Fine-tuned Model Integration):** Integrates and configures the domain-specific models for auto-drafting.
*   **30-06-PLAN.md (RAG-06: End-to-End Validation):** Comprehensive testing of the complete RAG pipeline.
