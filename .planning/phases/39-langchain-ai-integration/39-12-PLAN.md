<objective>
Implement the three judged eval dimensions AI-SPEC Section 5 reserves for where no deterministic oracle exists — questionnaire answer honesty (LLM-judge claim support), chat answer relevance & citation discipline (RAGAS faithfulness + answer_relevancy), and retrieval quality (RAGAS context precision/recall) — as nightly `eval and llm` tests. Combined with the six code-based dimensions in 39-11, this completes all eight AI-SPEC dimensions. These are the flywheel/nightly checks (they spend tokens and need the live gateway), not the merge gate, and they degrade gracefully when RAGAS or the gateway is absent.

Purpose: Close the eval coverage for the two grounding-quality dimensions and retrieval precision that code checks cannot measure, without adding a hosted dependency or blocking merges on token-spending tests.
Output: judges.py harness + three judged eval test files.
</objective>
