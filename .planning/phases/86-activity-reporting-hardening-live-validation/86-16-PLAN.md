A container-width switch would need a new hook that the scroll region makes unnecessary.

The plan also adds a structural vitest regression and fixes the cosmetic Organizations card chip overlap at 366 px and below. Both stay inside this small plan. Finally, it corrects the UI-SPEC table-budget arithmetic so the contract matches the code.
