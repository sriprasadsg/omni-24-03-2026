Make the plan decide a direct tenant's seats with a Super Admin override that survives plan changes (D-03, D-09, D-10, D-14 numbers), keep partner allotments out of it (D-11), give the platform organization no limit (D-41), keep over-limit tenants' running agents (D-12), and make every manual seat write an override: the licence PATCH number, approve-and-raise, and the partner-to-direct move (D-44).

Output: new seat functions in licensing.py, the announce helper, the approve-and-raise and move edits, and the seat tests.
</objective>
