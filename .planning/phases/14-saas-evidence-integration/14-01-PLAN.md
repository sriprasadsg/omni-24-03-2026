<objective>
Build the SaaS OAuth evidence integration layer so the platform can automatically pull compliance evidence from GitHub, Jira, Okta, Google Workspace, and Slack — removing the manual evidence burden for the most common control sources.

This is the #1 gap versus Comp AI and OpenLane Core: those platforms auto-collect evidence from the tools teams already use. Our platform currently requires manual upload for all SaaS-sourced evidence.
