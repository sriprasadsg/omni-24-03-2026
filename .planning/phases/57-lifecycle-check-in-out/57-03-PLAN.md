<objective>
Deliver the "are our records still true?" half of the phase: an admin marks an asset as
physically audited on a given date, and pulls a report of every asset overdue for audit.

Purpose: ITAM-LIFE-05 is what turns the assignment records built in 57-01 and 57-02 from claims
into verified facts. The report is deliberately computed at query time from an indexed date field
rather than by a background sweep, because this milestone's top recorded risk is exactly the
tenant-isolation bug class that background schedulers in this codebase keep reintroducing.

Output: `mark_asset_audited` and `overdue_audit_report` on the existing Phase-57 router,
`AuditMarkRequest` in `itam_models.py`, and the corresponding tests.

## Flagged Assumptions carried by this plan

The phase-wide spec-less-probe roll-up (all 6 items, no silent drops) lives in `57-01-PLAN.md`.
This plan is the named home for row 6:

- **Row 6 (ITAM-LIFE-05, unclassified).** The requirement text fixes neither the boundary
  semantics nor the fallback for an asset with no dates at all. The planner's answers, hardened
  as truths above but **not author-confirmed**, are: overdue means strictly older than
  now-minus-365-days (D-03's fixed 12 months expressed in days); an asset audited exactly at the
  cutoff instant is not yet overdue; never-audited falls back to `createdAt`; and an asset with
  neither date is **included** in the report under an explicit `ageBasis` of unknown with a null
  `daysOverdue`, rather than being dropped from the population or given a fabricated age.

That last choice is the load-bearing one. Every agent-discovered asset in this codebase has no
`createdAt` — `agent_registry_endpoints.py:123-149` writes `lastScanned` (refreshed on every
heartbeat, so it measures recency, not age) and never a creation date. Excluding those assets
would make a fleet look audited when most of it has never been physically verified; including
them as ordinary overdue rows would assert an age nobody knows. Labelling them is the only
option that neither hides nor invents.

**Report noise is the open risk.** On a fleet with many agent-discovered assets, the first run of
this report will be dominated by unknown-basis rows. That is honest but may be unusable, and the
right remedy — a backfill, a filter, or a separate "never verified" view — is a product decision,
not a planner decision. It is surfaced as the phase's one `<human-check>` in Task 2.
</flagged_assumptions>

  Compute the cutoff as `(datetime.now(timezone.utc) - timedelta(days=AUDIT_INTERVAL_DAYS)).isoformat(timespec="milliseconds")`
  so it carries the same `+00:00` offset and the same millisecond precision as every timestamp
  this phase writes, which is what makes lexicographic string comparison chronologically valid.

  suffix, but do not assume `createdAt` and `lastAuditedAt` are byte-identical in format.

- FastAPI matches routes by exact path template, so declaring the report route with a literal
  first segment guarantees it can never be captured by a parameterised sibling.
