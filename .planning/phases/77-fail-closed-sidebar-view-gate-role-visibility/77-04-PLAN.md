<objective>
Make MFA follow permissions instead of role names (D-14), so a custom role that grants admin-tier powers can never be a way around MFA, while every built-in role keeps exactly today's behaviour.

Purpose: custom roles (plan 77-03) are assignable and editable; without this, a tenant admin could hand out manage:rbac or manage:users without MFA (RESEARCH Pitfall 4, ACC-09).
Output: rbac_defaults.ADMIN_TIER_PERMISSIONS/requires_mfa, permission-based enforcement in authentication_service, mfa_action on login and /me, backend/tests/test_custom_role_mfa.py, the role-literal guard allowlist cleaned up.
</objective>
