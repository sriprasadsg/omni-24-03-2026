<objective>
Give the gate's recorded decisions their human and time-driven lifecycle: single-use expiring approvals (GATE-10, D-16), deny, undo that works during a stop (D-12), the circuit breaker that drops a tenant to Suggest (GATE-14, D-18), and the reconciler loop that expires approvals even during a stop (D-13), reconciles dispatched rows with their instruction outcome, and runs one at a time with lock renewal and per-tenant context reset (GATE-11).

Purpose: approvals must dispatch exactly once and never after expiry; the breaker and reconciler are the only time-driven state changes and must never cross tenants.
Output: three new package modules, the reconciler registration in app_startup, four test modules.
</objective>
