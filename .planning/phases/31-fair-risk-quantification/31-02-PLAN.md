<objective>
Add the FAIR frontend on top of Plan 31-01's proven `POST /api/risks/{id}/fair-simulation` contract. Because `RiskFormModal.tsx` is create-only today (single `onSubmit`, hardcoded "New Risk" title) and FAIR simulation inherently needs an EXISTING `risk_id`, the lightest-weight design consistent with CLAUDE.md's "nothing more than asked" is a NEW small `RiskFairModal.tsx` opened from a new row action in `RiskRegister.tsx` — this leaves the create-only `RiskFormModal` completely untouched (no edit-mode retrofit) and keeps every file well under the 500-line cap. FAIR display is additive and parallel to the existing qualitative Score column and stats.

Purpose: Give users a reachable, usable path to quantify a risk in dollar terms and see the loss-exceedance summary in the register they already use.
Output: `runFairSimulation()` in apiService, an extended `Risk` type, a new `RiskFairModal.tsx`, and a FAIR column + trigger wired into `RiskRegister.tsx`.
</objective>
