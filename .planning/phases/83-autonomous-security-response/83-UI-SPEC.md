# Phase 83 — UI Design Contract

> Visual and interaction contract for the Phase 83 additions to the **Autonomous Ops** screen: the `Needs your decision` section (incident cards with per-action Approve and Deny, Confirm isolation with its 4-hour revert countdown, the kill-then-quarantine card), Undo on Done rows, Suggested rows in volume, the `Response settings` panel (two-source switch, block length, watched services, plus the Super Admin all-organizations case), and the honest "nothing was sent" message on the swarm and SOAR paths. Generated by gsd-ui-researcher, verified by gsd-ui-checker.
> Locked inputs: 83-CONTEXT.md D-01..D-22 (screen decisions D-04, D-06, D-07, D-08, D-10, D-12, D-13, D-15, D-16, D-17, D-18, D-22). Nothing here re-opens them. Visual details the context left open are recorded as researcher defaults (section "Assumptions Recorded", IDs R1..R14).
> Extends, does not replace, `81-UI-SPEC.md` and `82-UI-SPEC.md`: tokens, the four-size and two-weight type scale, the spacing scale, chip anatomy and tones, banner anatomy, `EmptyStateCard`, `Modal` conventions and the state vocabulary are inherited as written. Only deltas are declared here.
> New infrastructure: none. No new library, no new token, no new colour, no new chip tone. Built from the primitives named in 81/82-UI-SPEC and the files read for this contract: `components/AutonomousOpsActivity.tsx`, `AutonomousOpsActivityList.tsx`, `AutonomousOpsStatusCard.tsx`, `components/Modal.tsx`, `components/icons.tsx`, `components/SwarmDashboard.tsx`, `services/autonomousOpsApi.ts`, `backend/autonomous_ops_endpoints.py`, `backend/autonomous_ops/messages.py`, `shared/autonomous-ops-banned-terms.json`.

## Color

Inherited from 81 and 82: dominant `--bg-main`, secondary `.card` surface, accent `primary-600`, destructive `red-600`, amber warning, blue info, neutral text. The 60/30/10 split is unchanged: the new section and panel are `.card` surfaces on the page canvas, accent stays small.

No tone is added. Phase 83 meanings map onto the existing chips:

| Meaning | Chip | Tone |
|---------|------|------|
| Waiting for a person (approval) | `Pending` | AMBER |
| Carried out, verified | `Done` | GREEN |
| Put back by a person | `Undone` | NEUTRAL |
| Sent, host reported failure or no result | `Failed` | RED |
| Not done by design (never-touch list, no redo for 24 h after an undo, the host's agent lacks support). One source where two are needed is a `Suggested` row, not `Refused`. | `Refused` | OUTLINE |
| One source only, vulnerability, file change | `Suggested` | BLUE |
| Countdown to an automatic outcome (`Expires in`, `Reverts in`) | the `Pending` chip anatomy with `ClockIcon` | AMBER |

Accent reserved for (the 81 list items 1 to 4 and the 82 item 5 stand; Phase 83 adds exactly four):
6. The `Approve` button on an action line (`bg-primary-600 hover:bg-primary-700 text-white`). It is the one affirmative decision on the screen, so it takes the accent the way `Resume automatic actions` does; the consequence is stated in words on the line, not signalled by red (default R3).
7. `Save settings` when the form has unsaved changes.
8. The confirm button of a dialog that lifts a safeguard (`Turn off the rule`, `Apply to every organization`), the same pattern as `Resume automatic actions`.
9. The switch track when it is on (the safe state, two sources required).

Accent is NOT used for: `Deny`, `Undo`, `Confirm isolation`, `Review in Needs your decision`, `Open patching`, `Show more incidents`, `Show more actions`, `Discard changes`, any chip, any countdown, or the section headings.

Destructive red is used in this phase only for the `Failed` chip, inline error text and error blocks. There is no new red button: nothing in this phase deletes data (quarantine moves, never deletes, RESP-03), and the "What happens" lines use the neutral `InfoIcon`, not a warning colour.

Meaning never relies on colour alone: every chip carries an icon (12px, `aria-hidden`) and visible text; the switch carries the words `On` and `Off`; a disabled Approve carries a visible reason line.

## Screens and States

### 1. Placement on the Autonomous Ops screen

Tenant scope (a tenant role, or platform staff viewing an organization), top to bottom: page header, status card (81), **Needs your decision (new, only when there is something to decide)**, **Response settings (new, collapsed card, holders of `manage:autonomous_ops` only)**, Activity section.

Super Admin in platform scope, top to bottom: page header, status card, Platform health (82), **Response settings for every organization (new, collapsed card)**, Activity. Approval cards are per organization, so platform scope has no decision section.

Platform organization (81 D-04): neither new section renders. Platform Support (read-only): sees `Needs your decision` with no buttons and never sees the settings panel.

**Visual anchor (focal point).** When at least one incident is open, the first incident card in `Needs your decision` is the anchor of the screen below the status card: it is the first block after the status card, it is the only place with filled accent buttons, and it carries the only countdown chips. When nothing is open the section is not rendered and the status card is the anchor (as in 81). The stop banner (amber) and the watchdog-silent banner (red) still outrank both. The settings card is subordinate: collapsed it is one quiet `.card` row, and it is never placed above the decision section.

### 2. `Needs your decision` section (ACTV-02, D-06, D-15, D-16, D-17)

`<section aria-labelledby="decisions-title" id="decisions">`, `space-y-6` between cards. Header row (`flex flex-wrap items-center justify-between gap-4`): left `h2` 18/600 `Needs your decision` (`tabIndex={-1}`); right 12/400 muted `{n} incidents` (`1 incident`). One 14/400 muted line under the h2: `Approvals expire after 24 hours and then count as denied. An isolated computer returns to the network after 4 hours unless you confirm it.`

What belongs here: every incident (one host, alerts until 1 hour passes with nothing new, D-16) that has at least one action waiting for a person, or an isolation that is done but not yet confirmed. A lone action is still a card with one action line. Automatic actions of the same incident that already ran are listed as context lines without buttons so the approver sees the whole picture (R4). Suggestions never appear here (section 5).

**Where each control lives (one rule, so no action has two buttons):** Approve, Deny and Confirm isolation exist only on incident cards. Undo exists only on Done rows in the Activity list. A Pending row in the Activity list has no decision button, only the link `Review in Needs your decision` (section 4).

Ordering: soonest deadline first (an approval's 24-hour expiry or an isolation's revert time), ties by incident start, newest first. Paging: 10 cards, then a secondary `Show more incidents` button (`min-h-10`, pending `Loading...`).

Data: one fetch on mount, every 30 s while the tab is visible, on focus, on the page `Refresh`, and immediately after any decision. A background poll shows no skeleton and no spinner. Cards that settle (every action decided, no unconfirmed isolation) stay in place showing their settled lines until the next poll or `Refresh`, then leave without animation.

Section states:

| State | Contract |
|-------|----------|
| First load | Nothing is rendered (no skeleton, no heading): the common case is an empty section and a skeleton would shift the page twice (R5). `aria-busy` is not used. |
| Nothing to decide | The section is not rendered at all. Under the `Pending` filter the Activity list's own empty copy applies. |
| Populated | Cards below. |
| Error (first load) | The heading row is rendered with a red block (`AlertTriangleIcon`, `role="alert"`): `Couldn't load approvals. Check your connection and try again.` and a `Try again` text button. Missing approvals must never be silent. |
| Error (background poll) | Keeps the last cards and shows nothing new (inherited rule). |
| Stopped | Amber block (`PauseCircleIcon`, `role="status"`) above the first card: `Approving is paused while automatic actions are stopped. You can still deny. Requests keep their 24-hour clock.` Every Approve and every Confirm isolation is `disabled` (D-12); Deny stays enabled. The block names the scope when it is the platform stop: `... stopped for the whole platform.` |
| Shadow mode | No approval cards exist: shadow mode records Simulation rows and nothing is pending. If a card is ever delivered while `status.shadow.effective` is true, its buttons are not rendered (a simulation has nothing to decide). |
| Live region | One visually hidden `role="status"` in the section announces `{n} incidents need your decision` only when the count rises after a background poll. It never announces on first load and never per poll. |

#### 2a. Incident card anatomy

`<article id="approval-{incidentId}" tabIndex={-1} aria-labelledby>`, `.card overflow-hidden`. Three zones.

**Header** (`flex flex-wrap items-start justify-between gap-4 px-6 py-4 border-b border-gray-200 dark:border-white/10`):
- Left (`min-w-0`, `flex items-start gap-4`): `ShieldAlertIcon` 20px `aria-hidden` `text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400`; then a column: host name 14/600 `break-words`; under it 12/400 muted `Started {relative} · {n} alerts` (`1 alert`), full timestamp in `title`.
- Right: the deadline chip (AMBER, `ClockIcon`): `Expires in 19 h`, `Expires in 42 min`, `Expires in under 1 min`; or, when the nearest deadline is an isolation revert, `Reverts in 3 h 12 min` (`Reverts in 12 min`, `Reverts in under 1 min`). The chip shows the nearest deadline among the card's actions; each action line also shows its own. Full local timestamp in `title`. A deadline in the past renders `Expired` with every button on that card `disabled` until the next refetch removes or updates it.

Countdown ticking: one `setInterval` of 30 s for the whole section (not per card, not per second), recomputing from the server's `expiresAt` and `revertsAt`; no live region on it.

**Action lines** (`<ul>` with `divide-y divide-gray-200 dark:divide-white/10`, each `<li class="px-6 py-4 flex flex-col gap-4">`). At `lg` (1024px) and wider an action line is two columns: left `min-w-0 flex-1`, right `shrink-0` for controls; below `lg` the controls drop under the text, left-aligned.

Left column, in this order:
1. Row of: state chip (`Pending`, `Done`, `Failed`, `Refused`; whichever the server bucket says) then action label 14/600 (`Kill process`, `Isolate host`, `Restart service`, `Reboot host`, `Block IP address`, `Quarantine file`) with `break-words`.
2. Target: 12/400 `font-mono` muted `break-all` (`powershell.exe (PID 4120)`, `C:\Users\Public\x.exe`, `203.0.113.7`, `nginx`).
3. Why: 14/400 muted `break-words`, the server reason text, then the rule id in `font-mono` 12/400 muted.
4. What happens (waiting actions only): 12/400 muted, `InfoIcon` 12px `aria-hidden`, `id` referenced by the buttons' `aria-describedby`. Server-supplied text (see Copywriting Contract, "What happens").
5. Result (decided or running actions): 14/400 `break-words`, the server result text (`Approved. Sent to {host}.`, `Denied by {name}.`, `Expired after 24 hours, counted as denied.`).

Right column (only for an action that is waiting and where the viewer holds approval access): `Approve` (accent) and `Deny` (secondary) side by side (`flex gap-2`), `min-h-10 px-4`. Button `aria-label` carries the target: `Approve: Kill process powershell.exe on {host}`; `Deny: ...`. Both are real `<button>`.

Button states (per action line, independent of other lines on the same card, D-17):

| State | Contract |
|-------|----------|
| Idle | Both enabled. |
| Approving | Approve label `Approving...`, both buttons `disabled`, repeated clicks send one request. |
| Denying | Deny label `Denying...`, both `disabled`. |
| Approve blocked by stop | Approve `disabled`; line helper 12/400 muted `Paused while automatic actions are stopped.` Deny enabled. |
| Expired (client clock or 409 `expired`) | Both replaced by the Result `This request has expired and counts as denied.` |
| Already decided by someone else (409 `already_handled`) | Buttons replaced by the server text `This decision was already made.` plus the refetched Result. |
| Success | Buttons replaced by the refetched Result; toast (see Copywriting); **focus moves to the action line container** (`tabIndex={-1}`, `role="status"`) so keyboard focus is not lost when the buttons disappear. |
| Failure | Line stays; red 14/400 `role="alert"` text under the buttons: `Couldn't approve this action: {server message}. Try again.` (`Couldn't deny this action: ...`). Fallback `Couldn't approve this action. Check your connection and try again.` Buttons re-enabled. |
| Viewer lacks approval access | No buttons. One 12/400 muted line: `Waiting for someone with approval access.` Platform Support: same line. |

**Confirm isolation (D-15, RESP-05).** An isolate action that is Done and not yet confirmed shows, on its line: the Done chip, the result text `{host} is isolated. The connection to the platform stays open.`, an AMBER countdown chip `Reverts in 3 h 12 min`, and a 12/400 muted consequence line `Confirming keeps this computer isolated until you restore it. Without it, the computer returns to the network automatically at {HH:MM}.` Right column: one secondary button `Confirm isolation` (`min-h-10`, `LockIcon` 16px left), one click, no dialog, disabled during a stop (same blocked line as Approve). Pending label `Confirming...`. Success toast `Isolation confirmed. {host} stays isolated until you restore it.` and the line shows `Confirmed by {name} at {HH:MM}. The computer stays isolated until a person restores it.` Revert happened (nobody confirmed): the action leaves the card on the next refetch and its Activity row reads `Returned to the network automatically after 4 hours. Nobody confirmed the isolation.` with the `Undone` chip. A confirmed isolation can be put back from its Activity row with `Undo` (hint `Restores the network connection.`).

**Kill-then-quarantine card (D-06).** One action line, not two, because it is one decision: label `Kill process, then quarantine file`; target shows both: process (mono) then the file path (mono) on its own line. Above the What happens line, a blue info block (`rounded-lg border p-4 text-sm`, `InfoIcon` 16px): `The file couldn't be moved because it is running. Approving ends the process, then tries the quarantine again. Nothing is deleted.` After approval, the line shows an ordered step list (`<ol>`, 14/400, each step an icon plus text, server state per step): `Kill process` and `Quarantine file`, each with Pending clock, Done check or Failed x. If the kill worked and the quarantine failed again: step 1 Done, step 2 Failed with the reason, and the result `The process was ended but the file is still in place: {reason}.` No further automatic try.

**Restart after the budget is used up (D-11).** An action line `Restart service` with Why `Restarted {service} 3 times in the last hour. Further restarts need your approval.` and What happens `Restarts {service} on this computer. If it fails again it stays down until you act.`

**Long cards.** More than 5 action lines: the first 5 render and a text button `Show {n} more actions` (14/600, underline, `aria-expanded`, `ChevronDownIcon`) expands the rest in place; `Show fewer actions` collapses. Actions that need a person sort first, then context lines newest first.

### 3. Activity list: Undo on Done rows (ACTV-02)

Layout decision (R6): **no sixth column.** The five column minimums of the Phase 81 table already sum to 1016px (`w-40` 160 + `w-56` 224 + 240 + `w-48` 192 + 200), so an Actions column would force horizontal scroll at the `xl` breakpoint. Row actions render inside the **Result** cell (table) and the Result `dd` (stacked cards), under the result text, `mt-2 flex flex-wrap items-start gap-2`. The `Pending` filter, the other filters, chips and the stacked-card layout are unchanged.

Undo shows on a row when the server says `canUndo` is true (the action has an inverse, it is Done, it has not already been undone or expired, the viewer holds approval access and is not Platform Support). The client never infers this (inherited rule). Never on Simulation rows (nothing was done), never on Pending, Failed, Refused, Suggested or control rows.

- Button: secondary, `min-h-10 px-4`, `RefreshCcwIcon` 16px left, label `Undo`, `aria-label` `Undo: {action label} on {host}`. Under it 12/400 muted `{undoHint}` from the server: `Removes the block on {ip}.`, `Restores {path} to where it was.`, `Restores the network connection.`
- One click, no confirmation dialog (the roadmap's "one click"; the hint states what happens, and every undo is itself recorded). Pending label `Undoing...`, disabled, one request per click.
- Allowed during a stop (D-12): not disabled by a stop.
- Success: toast `Undo sent to {host}.`; the row is replaced by the refetched row (state `Undone` and its result). If the response carries `breakerTripped: true`, show instead `Undo sent to {host}. Several automatic actions in a row were undone or failed, so this organization dropped to Suggest only. A person with manage access can restore it.` (info toast) and refetch the status card.
- Failure: red 12/400 `role="alert"` under the button: `Couldn't undo this action: {server message}. Try again.` Fallback `Couldn't undo this action. Check your connection and try again.` 409 texts come from the server (`This action cannot be undone.`, `This action is not in a state that can be undone.`) and the row refetches.
- Focus after success: moved to the row's Result cell (`tabIndex={-1}`).

Actions with no inverse (restart service, kill process, reboot): no Undo button, no placeholder text, no disabled button. The Result text carries the outcome (D-12): `Restarted {service}. Healthy again after {s} s.`

Pending rows (all of them, grouped or not) show in the Result cell the server result text then a text button `Review in Needs your decision` (14/600, underline). It scrolls to and focuses `#approval-{incidentId}`. If the card is not rendered (the viewer lacks approval access or the card is paged out), the link is omitted and the plain result text stays.

Block rows (RESP-02): while active the Result reads `Blocked {ip} until {Mon D, HH:MM}. Verified: the firewall rule is in place.` (24-hour local time, full timestamp in `title`). When time runs out the row keeps its `Done` chip, loses Undo, and the Result becomes `Block removed automatically at {HH:MM} when its time ran out.` A still-flagged address becomes a new row through the gate (D-14); nothing extends the old one.

|-----|----------|-----|--------|

| Block, check failed | `Block IP address` | same | `The host did not confirm the firewall rule, so this is marked failed.` (Failed) |
| Block expired | `Block IP address` | same | `Block removed automatically at {HH:MM} when its time ran out.` |

| Restart (Done) | `Restart service` / `Automatic` | `{Service} stopped and is on your watched list.` | `Restarted {service}. Healthy again after {s} s. Restart {k} of 3 this hour.` |

| One source only (Suggested) | `Suggested: block IP address` or `Suggested: quarantine file` | `Only one source flagged this: {origin}. A second, different source has to agree.` | `Nothing was done. If a second source agrees within 24 hours this runs on its own. After that it stays a suggestion.` |

Origin names shown to people (D-01): `agent verdict`, `URLhaus`, `MalwareBazaar`, `OTX`, `VirusTotal`, `detection on this computer`. Two entries from one origin never read as "two sources".

### 5. Suggested rows in volume (D-07, D-08, D-03)

Goal: a host that reports hundreds of vulnerabilities must not produce hundreds of rows, and suggestions must never push a decision out of sight.

Rules:
1. **Separate surfaces.** Decisions live in `Needs your decision` above the list; suggestions live only in Activity. No suggestion volume can move a card.
2. **One row per host per suggestion kind**, never one per finding. The server groups: `vulnerability` (D-07, covers both routing routes), `file_change` (D-08) and `single_signal` (D-03, one row per indicator, so these stay few). A newer sweep updates the existing row's count and time instead of adding a row (the row keeps one `createdAt`, the time of the latest sweep).
3. **Compact by default.** A grouped row uses the normal five cells; the extra weight is behind a disclosure:

| Cell | Vulnerability row | File-change row |
|------|-------------------|-----------------|
| State | `Suggested` chip, time | `Suggested` chip, time |
| What ran | `Suggested: patch vulnerabilities`; under it `Suggestion only` | `Suggested: review file changes`; under it `Suggestion only` |
| Why | `{n} known vulnerabilities reported by {host}. Highest severity: {severity}.` | `{n} files changed on {host} since the last baseline.` |
| Target | host 14/600; under it `{n} vulnerabilities` or the top three CVE ids in mono, `+{k} more` | host; under it up to three paths in mono, `+{k} more` |
| Result | `No action was taken. Patching is where you apply fixes.` + text link `Open patching` (`ExternalLinkIcon` 12px) + `Show details` toggle | `No action was taken. Check whether these changes were expected.` + `Open file integrity` link + `Show details` toggle |

4. **Details disclosure** (text button `Show details` / `Hide details`, 14/600, `aria-expanded`, `ChevronRightIcon` that rotates, no animation beyond the global rule): expands inside the Result cell a `ul` of at most 10 items (CVE id mono plus package name, or path mono), then the line `Showing 10 of {n}. Open patching to see all of them.` (link). Items are server data capped at 10; no client paging.
5. **Links.** `Open patching` navigates to `patchManagement` (permission `view:patching`); `Open file integrity` to `fimMonitoring` (`view:fim`). The screen gets an `onOpenView` prop (default undefined) from `App.tsx`; each link renders as a link only when the viewer can open that view, otherwise as plain text without the link. ## Accessibility and Interaction Notes

- Focus: global `:focus-visible` ring only. After Approve, Deny, Confirm isolation or Undo succeeds, the buttons disappear, so focus moves to the line or Result cell (`tabIndex={-1}`); it is never left on a removed node. `Review in Needs your decision` moves focus to `#approval-{id}`.
- Live regions: errors are `role="alert"`; the section count announcement is polite and only on a rise after a poll; the settled action line is `role="status"`; countdowns and polls are silent.
- Card semantics: `<article>` with the host name as its accessible name; action lines are a `<ul>`; step lists are an `<ol>`.
- Buttons name their target (`Approve: Kill process powershell.exe on {host}`); a disabled Approve is explained by visible text referenced with `aria-describedby`.
- Switch: `role="switch"`, `aria-checked`, visible `On` or `Off` text, Space and Enter toggle.
- Disclosures (`Show details`, `Show more actions`, the settings header): real `<button>` with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- Chips carry icon plus text; countdown text is plain words, never colour-only.
- Motion: only `animate-spin` and `animate-pulse`; the chevron rotation uses no transition beyond the global reduced-motion rule; no entrance animation on cards.
- Touch targets: Approve, Deny, Confirm isolation, Undo, Save, Add and dialog confirms `min-h-10`; remove-chip buttons 32px.
- Times: `<time dateTime>`; clock times `HH:MM` 24-hour local, full local timestamp in `title`.
- Text fit: no `truncate` on any data (hosts, paths, reasons, services). Paths, IPs, process names and service names use `break-all`; prose uses `break-words`.
- Security of rendered strings: every server string (host, path, process name, reason, service name, origin) is rendered as React text; no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`, no link-ification of targets or reasons. Only `Open patching` and `Open file integrity` navigate, through `onOpenView`.
- Idempotency: every decision, undo, confirm and save button is disabled while its request is pending, so one click sends one request; the server's single-use approval is the second line of defence.
- Permissions: a button the viewer cannot use is not rendered (inherited); Platform Support never gets a decision control; API keys are never a UI actor.
