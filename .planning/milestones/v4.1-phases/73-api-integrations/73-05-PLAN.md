## Flagged Assumptions

These are carried into execution explicitly rather than resolved silently. Each must be restated in the plan summary with what was actually found.

1. **RESEARCH.md Assumption A2 is false, and this plan proceeds on a substitute.** A2 assumed D-10's "high-value" asset request maps to an existing cost-style field on the asset-request model. Planning checked `backend/itam_models.py` lines 571-596 directly: `AssetRequestBase` carries only `item_description`, `quantity` and `reason`, and `AssetRequest` adds only identity, status and approval fields. **There is no cost field of any kind.** A cost-only filter would therefore match zero requests in every existing tenant, making the trigger dead on arrival. Task 2 resolves this by adding an optional `estimated_cost` field (additive, absent by default, never required) and classifying a request as high-value when either that field is present and at or above a fixed cost constant, or its quantity is at or above a fixed quantity constant. Both constants are fixed, matching the user's resolution of research Open Question 3 (fixed constant, not tenant-configurable). If the reviewer disagrees with the quantity fallback, that is a scoping conversation to have before this plan executes — do not silently drop it, because dropping it makes the trigger unreachable.

2. **Edge-probe item ITAM-API-02 is unclassified and remains unresolved.** The deterministic edge engine could not classify what boundary condition ITAM-API-02 ("webhook system for events") most needs proving. Planning's reading is that the dominant unclassified risk is *delivery-time* behaviour that this phase does not own — retry policy, ordering guarantees, and subscriber back-pressure all live in the pre-existing `webhook_service` dispatcher, which this phase reuses unmodified and does not test. Recorded as unresolved, not backstopped.

3. **Edge-probe item ITAM-API-03 is unclassified and remains unresolved.** Same treatment. Planning's reading is that the dominant unclassified risk is third-party behaviour outside this codebase: what a Jira or ServiceNow instance does with a duplicate, malformed, or rate-limited create call. The dedup guard and the non-re-raising failure contract bound the blast radius, but the third party's own behaviour is not verified by any test in this phase. Recorded as unresolved, not backstopped.
