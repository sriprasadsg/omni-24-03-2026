## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-71-05 | Tampering | Warranty expiry date/Depreciation values | high | mitigate | Input validation: `warranty_expiry_date` must be a future date. `salvage_value` must be non-negative and less than purchase price. `useful_life_years` must be positive. RBAC on asset updates. |
| T-71-06 | Information Disclosure | Warranty/Depreciation data | medium | mitigate | Tenant isolation enforced for all asset data. |
| T-71-07 | Denial of Service | Scheduled warranty task | medium | mitigate | Implement robust error handling and logging in the scheduled task. Rate-limit notification sends to prevent abuse. Ensure task runs with minimal privileges. |
| T-71-08 | Elevation of Privilege | Notification content injection | low | mitigate | Sanitize any user-generated content included in notifications to prevent XSS or similar injection attacks if notifications are rendered in a UI. |
| T-71-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | high | mitigate | slopcheck + blocking human checkpoint for [ASSUMED]/[SUS]. |
</threat_model>
