<objective>
Complete the hand-off loop: an admin checks a deployed asset back in, returning it to stock and
clearing its assignment, and can read the whole append-only trail of hand-offs for any asset.

Purpose: ITAM-LIFE-03 closes the round trip that 57-01 opened, and the history read is what makes
ITAM-LIFE-04's trail actually *visible per asset* rather than merely written. This plan also pins
the three edge semantics the spec-less edge probe surfaced for ITAM-LIFE-04 — what happens with
no entries, with identical timestamps, and with ties in the sort order.

Output: `checkin_asset` and `list_assignment_history` on the existing Phase-57 router,
`CheckinRequest` in `itam_models.py`, and the corresponding tests.
</objective>

`57-01-PLAN.md` under `<flagged_assumptions>`. This plan is the named home for four of those

- **Row 2 (ITAM-LIFE-03, unclassified).** Check-in is gated on the asset being currently

  fields and deliberately retains `locationId`. Neither behaviour is stated by the requirement
  text — both are planner judgment, not author-confirmed.
- **Row 3 (ITAM-LIFE-04, adjacency).** Identical timestamps never merge or collide; both rows

- **Row 4 (ITAM-LIFE-04, empty).** No history is 200 plus an empty list; an unresolvable or

- **Row 5 (ITAM-LIFE-04, ordering).** Ties on `ts` are broken by `_id` descending, giving a
  deterministic total order.

the history read route is the surface where a "let me just fix that note" affordance would
