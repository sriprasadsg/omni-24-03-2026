<objective>
Make an asset's warranty visible: derive its expiry from the purchase date and warranty period recorded in Plan 59-01, classify it against a per-tenant configurable alert window, and expose both through a read-only, RBAC-gated, tenant-scoped route.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 has two halves — "Admin sees each asset's warranty status/expiry" and "receives an alert". This plan delivers the first half and, more importantly, builds the single status function the second half depends on. Plan 59-04's background sweep calls the very same `compute_warranty_status` and the very same `get_warranty_alert_window`, so the status an operator sees on screen and the condition that triggers their alert are one definition, not two that can drift apart.

Output: `_add_months`, `compute_warranty_status` and `get_warranty_alert_window` in `backend/itam_finance_service.py`; `GET /api/assets/{asset_id}/warranty` in `backend/itam_finance_endpoints.py`; and `backend/tests/test_itam_finance_warranty.py`.

Depends on Plan 59-01, which creates both files, persists `purchaseDate`/`warrantyMonths`, and establishes the tenant-isolated lookup and test-fixture conventions this plan extends.
</objective>

Add `_add_months(dt: datetime, months: int) -> datetime`. Compute `total = dt.month - 1 + months`, `year = dt.year + total // 12`, `month = total % 12 + 1`, then clamp the day with `min(dt.day, calendar.monthrange(year, month)[1])` before returning `dt.replace(year=year, month=month, day=day)`. The clamp is the whole point: a warranty purchased on the 31st and running for one month must land on the last real day of the following month, because `dt.replace(month=2, day=31)` raises `ValueError` and a raised exception inside a background sweep would be swallowed by its outer handler and silently stop every alert. Use stdlib `calendar` — do not introduce `dateutil`, `arrow` or `pendulum` for this; the whole helper is six lines and RESEARCH's Don't-Hand-Roll table explicitly rules out a date library for this phase.
