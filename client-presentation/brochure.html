What is Omni-Agent?
A fully integrated, AI-native cybersecurity operating system that deploys autonomous agents across your entire infrastructure — collecting telemetry, hunting threats, remediating vulnerabilities, and orchestrating response without human intervention. A single platform replacing five or more point solutions.

🤖
Autonomous Agent Fleet — Self-healing, self-updating agents deployed on every endpoint. Agents execute playbooks, report telemetry, and participate in swarm coordination without centralised polling.
🛡
Unified SOC — EDR, SIEM, UEBA, and SOAR in one pane of glass. Correlated alerts from all sources reduce analyst alert fatigue and accelerate mean-time-to-contain.
