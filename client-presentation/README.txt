HOW TO OPEN
-----------

  INTERACTIVE BOOK (index.html)
  - Double-click index.html, or open it in Chrome / Edge / Firefox.
  - Controls:
      * Click the right page (or right arrow button) to turn forward.
      * Click the left page (or left arrow button) to turn backward.
      * Press → or ← keyboard arrow keys.
      * Swipe left/right on touch screens.
      * Click any dot in the nav bar to jump directly to a section.
  - Works fully offline — no internet connection required.

  BROCHURE / PDF (brochure.html or .pdf)
  - Open brochure.html in Chrome or Edge.
  - Click the "Save as PDF / Print" button at the top.
  - In the print dialog: choose "Save as PDF", Paper = A4, Margins = None,
    Background graphics = ON.  Then save.
  - Alternatively, open the pre-exported Enterprise-Omni-Agent-Platform.pdf
    directly in any PDF viewer.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
-------------------
  - Any modern browser: Chrome 100+, Edge 100+, Firefox 100+, Safari 15+.
  - No server, no internet, no installation needed.
  - Recommended display resolution: 1280x800 or larger for the interactive book.
  - On smaller screens the book scales automatically.
